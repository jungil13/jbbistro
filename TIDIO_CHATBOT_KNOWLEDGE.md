### How can I pay for my reservation?

Available payment options are shown during checkout. These may include online payment through PayMongo or Pay on Arrival. Pay-on-arrival reservations may require manager confirmation.

Not always. Confirmation depends on your payment method and reservation status. Pay-on-arrival bookings may need manager confirmation. Check your account or confirmation email for updates.

### Where can I check my reservation status?

Sign in and open [My Profile](https://www.jbenzbistro.shop/profile) to check your reservations and notifications. You can also check your confirmation email.

### How do I request a refund?

Sign in and open [My Profile](https://www.jbenzbistro.shop/profile). Find the eligible reservation payment and submit a refund request with a reason. The bistro will review your request. Submitting a request does not mean it has been approved or processed.

### Which payments can I request a refund for online?

The online refund request process is for eligible verified or paid PayMongo payments. If you do not see an option to request a refund, or you need help with another payment type, call 09935343043 or email jbenzbistro@gmail.com.

### How long does a refund take?

After a refund is approved and processed, it usually takes **3 to 5 working days** to appear, depending on the payment method. The timing may vary by payment provider or bank.

### What do the refund statuses mean?

- **Pending review:** The bistro has received your request and has not made a decision yet.
- **Approved:** The request was approved, but the refund still needs to be processed.
- **Processing / Awaiting PayMongo:** The payment provider is processing or confirming the refund.
- **Completed:** The refund was confirmed as processed. It may still take 3 to 5 working days to appear, depending on the payment method.
- **Rejected:** The request was not approved. Check My Profile for any decision notes.
- **Failed:** The payment provider could not complete the refund. Contact the bistro for help.

### Where can I check my refund status?

Sign in and open [My Profile](https://www.jbenzbistro.shop/profile). Refund status updates are shown with your account notifications. You may also receive updates by email.

### Can the chatbot make a reservation or process a refund for me?

No. The chatbot can explain how to use the website, but it cannot access customer accounts, change reservations, approve refund requests, or process payments. Sign in to manage your bookings, or contact the bistro for assistance.
